A quick guide

Small rules. Big laughs.

The basics

  • Two teams, with at least two players each. Keep team sizes equal, or within one player if the total is odd.
  • Choose a shared deck of exactly 50 character cards. These same cards are used in all three rounds.
  • Teams alternate 60-second turns. A rotating clue giver helps their teammates guess the card.
  • Each correct guess earns one point and removes the card for the rest of the round.
  • Unsolved and passed cards stay in the deck. When time runs out, hand the screen to the next team.
  • A round ends when every card is guessed. After round three, the highest total score wins. Equal scores are a tie.

Round 1

Describe

Unlimited clues. Do not say the character's name or any part of it. Passing is not allowed.

Round 2

One word

Only one word may be spoken per card, never the character's name or a part of it. Passing is allowed.

Round 3

Mime

No speaking or sound effects. Act out the character. Passing is allowed.

Shared-screen etiquette

Only the clue giver should see the active card. Clue restrictions are enforced by your fellow players, not speech recognition. The timer keeps running when you navigate away or refresh.

Progress is saved on this browser when local storage is available. No account, server, or network connection is needed after the app loads. Clearing browser data deletes your saved progress and custom decks.
